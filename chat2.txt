Okay, good instinct, and you're right to push for real pain points here. So the strongest current thread is, finance work is spread across too many systems, so people act as the glue. Analysts chasing data, Ops teams checking exceptions, customers juggling apps.

In consumer finance, no single app knows you. Your cards, your investments,taxes, bills. An agent that spans those silos, could answer, "Can I afford this trip?", instead of, here's your balance. That's coordination. Not just chat. In lending, banks still stitch documents, eligibility, fraud checks, and underwriting. An agent can prep files, flag missing info, request docs, and hand over a clean case to a human. In financial operations, inside companies, agents can chase approvals, check budgets, detect duplicates, and schedule payments, leaving humans for exceptions. For your demo, don't pitch "chatbot plus finance". Pitch "orchestration". Finance is a coordination problem.That framing stands out.
